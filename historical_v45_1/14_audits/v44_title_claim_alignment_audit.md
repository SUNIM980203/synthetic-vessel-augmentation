# v44 Title-Claim Alignment Audit

## Classification

MINOR RISK - NO TITLE CHANGE RECOMMENDED.

## Evidence actually supported

The observed Unity effect differed between the two prespecified, scope-specific adaptation regimens. The body states repeatedly that head-only and full-network used different, Duplicate-developed learning rates and that the contrast estimates regimen dependence rather than the causal effect of freezing alone.

## Concession

The experiment does not isolate a learning-rate-held-constant freezing effect. A reviewer could read “Adaptation-Scope Dependence” more narrowly than the manuscript intends.

## Strongest remaining defensible conclusion

“Dependence” remains a defensible descriptive title term because the measured effect differed across regimens organized by adaptation scope; the title does not say “causal effect of freezing.” The explicit regimen qualification in Abstract, Methods, Discussion, Limitations, and Conclusion preempts the stronger causal reading. The residual semantic risk is minor and does not materially justify renaming the established title.
