# v44 Reviewer Attack-Defense Matrix

| Reviewer attack | What is true | What the manuscript concedes | What the evidence still supports | Residual risk | Intrinsic vs writing-fixable |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Historical validation reuse | The primary interaction reused the historical validation scenes used for common-checkpoint selection. | It is neither untouched nor fully independent. | Reuse is shared across conditions and is not Unity-specific leakage; the bounded within-split interaction remains interpretable. | Moderate | Intrinsic evidence limitation |
| Globally untouched confirmation | xView test, HRSC, and DIOR appeared in earlier efficacy stages. | The v48 estimates are post-lock but not globally unseen. | They are secondary post-lock transfer evidence. | Moderate | Intrinsic evidence limitation |
| Scope versus learning-rate regimen | Scope and separately developed learning rate varied jointly. | Freezing alone is not causally isolated. | The contrast supports dependence across the tested scope-specific adaptation regimens. | High | Intrinsic evidence limitation |
| Seed CI interpretation | Ten paired seeds repeat stochastic training on fixed evaluation sets. | The CIs do not sample scenes, images, or domains. | They quantify training-stochasticity variability conditional on each fixed split. | Moderate | Writing preempted |
| Sparse positive-scene uncertainty | Only four of nine validation and four of nine test scenes contain vessels. | Scene-population precision is limited. | The existing scene bootstrap is a sparse-cluster sensitivity analysis, not a population CI. | High | Intrinsic evidence limitation |
| Object-level support-reference weighting | The implementation uses object rows with cross-scene exclusion and no equal-scene weighting. | Scenes with more vessels can contribute more empirical mass. | The representation screen remains a disclosed object-level diagnostic. | Moderate | Writing preempted; redesign requires experiment |
| Support-scale confounding | Original Medium/Small efficacy distributions differed in scale and support. | Support is not independently causal. | The ranking of two fixed distributions remains descriptive configuration evidence. | High | Intrinsic evidence limitation |
| YOLO-specific support representation | The same YOLO family supplied features and the strongest efficacy result. | Support is not detector-independent realism. | It is a detector-specific screening signal. | High | Intrinsic evidence limitation |
| Faster R-CNN non-replication | The tested Faster R-CNN primary gate failed. | Generalization across architectures is not established. | The negative boundary is explicit and limits the claim to the tested configuration. | High | Intrinsic evidence limitation |
| RealCutout imperfection | Heuristic alpha masks, source context, omitted pixels, resizing, and compositing artifacts remain possible. | It is not a pure appearance-only control. | It still matches host, placement, target geometry, count, exposure, and much of the compositing path. | Moderate | Intrinsic evidence limitation |
| Small effect size | The paired effects are small. | No operational-importance or SOTA claim is made. | A bounded seed-consistent effect within the tested regimen remains supported. | High | Intrinsic evidence limitation |
| Low DIOR absolute AP | Absolute DIOR AP is low. | Transfer relevance and practical utility are limited. | The paired comparison is retained as secondary transfer evidence only. | High | Intrinsic evidence limitation |
| 0.50 threshold interpretation | The Tenengrad maximum was frozen before the final historical-frame outcome. | Its theory is not recoverable and it is not universal. | It is a protocol-specific operational acceptance criterion whose failure is preserved. | Moderate | Writing preempted |
| Prospective terminology | Earlier efficacy results were known. | The study was not globally preregistered or outcome-naive. | Each named feasibility procedure/rule preceded its corresponding new outcome. | Moderate | Writing preempted |
| Post-result analyses | Scene and Tenengrad diagnostics followed efficacy/gate outcomes. | They are sensitivity or explanatory evidence, not confirmation. | They can localize fragility without changing frozen decisions. | Moderate | Writing preempted |
| Reproducibility and unavailable artifacts | Licensed imagery, checkpoints, raw predictions, embeddings, and local historical inputs are not publicly redistributed. | The package is not fully independently recomputable. | A sanitized archive can remain auditable through hashes, manifests, aggregate results, protocols, and scripts. | High | Intrinsic evidence limitation plus author action |

No row is defended with an unrun analysis. Where an answer would require new evidence, the row is labeled an intrinsic evidence limitation.
