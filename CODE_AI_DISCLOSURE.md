# AI Assistance Disclosure

The manuscript Acknowledgment describes AI assistance in preparing the article and its Supplement. This document also provides additional detail on assistance used to prepare the public research package and its verification utilities.

## Manuscript and Supplement Preparation

OpenAI Codex assisted with English-language editing, rephrasing, limited passage restructuring, and reference checks during preparation of the manuscript and its Supplement.

## Document and Figure Preparation

Codex assisted with document and figure preparation, including overview layout and presentation-only exports from retained samples and numerical results. No experimental imagery or scientific result was generated for these figures. This statement concerns the preparation of those figures; it is not a project-wide declaration that AI was absent from other research activities.

## Public Research Package Preparation

OpenAI Codex assisted with file selection, documentation, metadata editing, path redaction, integrity indexing, and the creation and execution of the two CPU-only public verification utilities in scripts/. These release utilities were newly generated with AI assistance; they are not the frozen original scientific pipeline. They perform package-integrity and seed-summary verification.

During the original public-package preparation, no training, checkpoint selection, AP evaluation or retuning was run. Seed contrasts were checked and their summary intervals recomputed from retained public aggregate CSVs. These recorded release-preparation activities are distinct from this disclosure-alignment edit, which does not recompute statistics.

## Author Responsibility

The authors determined the scientific content and conclusions, reviewed and revised the AI-assisted material, and take responsibility for the final manuscript and accompanying materials.

## Historical Scope and Records

This package does not make a blanket claim that AI was absent from historical code development or analysis. The exact historical division of labor is not independently established by the release preparation or this wording alignment. The current manuscript is the wording reference, not new proof of every past activity. Original logs, frozen scripts, historical disclosures and provenance records are preserved. The distinction between the public verification utilities and the original scientific pipeline does not imply that all original code was exclusively human-authored.
